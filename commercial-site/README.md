# Costa Cleaning Co. commercial site

Static, commercial-only website for Costa Cleaning Co. (Fort Myers, FL): 16 pages generated by `build/build.py` from `build/content.py`.

- `site/` is the deployable output (Vercel root directory). `site/api/lead.js` emails the quote form through Resend (needs `RESEND_API_KEY`). The preview noindex header was removed at go-live.
- Build: `python3 -I build/build.py <this folder> "<path to costa-mark.svg>" tools/node_modules/lucide-static/icons` (run `npm install` in `tools/` first).
- QA: `python3 build/qa.py site qa` (Playwright, Chrome and axe-core).
- Map: `tools/geo/map3.mjs` writes `build/map.json` from @geo-maps/earth-lands-1km (OpenStreetMap data, ODbL) and US county outlines.
- The lead form posts to `/api/lead`.
- Photo and map credits are on `site/credits.html`; the photo licenses (CC BY / CC BY-SA) require that credit while the stand-in photos are used.
