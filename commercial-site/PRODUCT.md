## Users

Commercial buyers in Southwest Florida who already know they need a cleaning company and are comparing or replacing one:
- office and facility managers,
- medical and dental practice managers,
- retail and restaurant managers,
- property managers of commercial buildings and suites.

Many are under contract with a cleaner that went quiet, missed work, or rotated crews. They read on a phone between tasks and on a desktop at work. They decide on reliability, communication, proof the work was done, scheduling around their hours, and a fixed price.

## Product Purpose

Costa Cleaning Co. is a Fort Myers commercial cleaning company. The site exists to turn commercial buyers into booked walkthroughs and phone calls. Success means walkthrough requests and calls from commercial accounts, not residential.

## Positioning

Five commitments a buyer can hold Costa to: Communication, Accountability, Dependability, Quality, Accommodating. The work happens when the client is closed, so the site has to show what happens while nobody is watching. Content strategy is delegated to Claude ("use your intelligence to decide what content should be", Nov 15, 2026).

## Operating Context

- Cleaning happens before opening, after closing or on weekends.
- How a buyer starts: a free walkthrough, on site or by video, then one flat-rate price in writing, then recurring visits. For buyers switching vendors, the start date is planned around their current notice period.

## Product Principles

- Show how the service runs, never adjectives about it.
- Every promise is a commitment Costa can be held to, phrased so a buyer could quote it back.
- Answer the buyer's real question first, then offer one action: walkthrough or call.
- Local and specific: Southwest Florida buildings, hours and cities.
