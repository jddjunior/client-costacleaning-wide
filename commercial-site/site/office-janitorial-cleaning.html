Office and janitorial cleaning questions

Answers to what buyers ask before a walkthrough.

Do you clean during business hours?

Most offices are cleaned after close or before opening, so your team never works around a crew. Daytime visits can be arranged.

Do you bring supplies and equipment?

Yes. We bring our own equipment, including HEPA-filter vacuums, and eco-friendly cleaning products. Ask about paper and soap restocking at your walkthrough.

Can we change our schedule later?

Yes. Tell your point of contact and we adjust it. Your flat rate is re-quoted in writing only if the scope changes.

How is the price set?

After a free walkthrough we send one flat rate in writing, based on your square footage, how often we clean and the rooms in scope.
