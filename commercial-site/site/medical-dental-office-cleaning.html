Medical and dental office cleaning questions

Answers to what buyers ask before a walkthrough.

Call (239) 980-6084
Do you clean while patients are in the building?

No. We clean early in the morning, after the last appointment or on weekends. Daytime visits can be arranged if your practice needs them.

Do you handle medical waste?

No. Regulated medical waste and sharps stay with your licensed medical waste hauler. We handle regular trash and recycling.

Which surfaces do you disinfect?

Door handles, light switches, counters, chairs, exam-table surfaces and restroom fixtures, listed room by room in your checklist.

Will the same people clean our practice each time?

Yes. One crew learns your rooms and routines, with trained backups when someone is out.
