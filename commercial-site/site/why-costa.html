What one visit looks like

The same four steps on every visit, at every building.

  1. Before the crew arrives

    Your point of contact confirms the crew, the checklist and any notes you sent that day.

  2. During the visit

    The same crew works room by room through your written checklist, after you close or before you open.

  3. Before they leave

    Every line on the checklist is done, and doors, lights and alarms are left the way you asked.

  4. If anything was missed

    Tell your contact. The fix is scheduled at no charge and added to the checklist so it does not happen again.

Questions before you switch

Straight answers on price, crews, scheduling and switching.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. We keep accounts by keeping our commitments.

Are your crews background-checked and insured?

Yes. Costa Cleaning Co. is licensed and insured, and every crew member is background-checked. A certificate of insurance is available on request.

Will the same crew clean our building?

Yes. A trained crew is assigned to your site, with trained backups for sick days and vacations. We tell you before any change.

What happens if something gets missed?

Tell your point of contact. We schedule the fix at no charge and note it on your checklist so it does not happen again.

Can you clean before we open or after we close?
