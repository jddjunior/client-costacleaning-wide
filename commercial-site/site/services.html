Questions about our services

Straight answers on price, crews, scheduling and switching.

Call (239) 980-6084
How is pricing set?

After a free FaceTime video walkthrough, we send one flat rate in writing based on your square footage, how often we clean and the rooms in scope. No hourly billing.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. We keep accounts by keeping our commitments.

Are your crews background-checked and insured?

Yes. Costa Cleaning Co. is licensed and insured, and every crew member is background-checked. A certificate of insurance is available on request.

Will the same crew clean our building?

Yes. A trained crew is assigned to your site, with trained backups for sick days and vacations. We tell you before any change.
