Who we are

Costa Cleaning Co. cleans offices, medical and dental practices, retail stores, restaurants, gyms, warehouses, vacant suites and construction sites across Southwest Florida.

Most of our work happens when our clients are closed, so the standard has to hold when nobody is watching. That is why every account gets one point of contact, a checklist written for its building and the same five commitments in writing.

What we put in writing

Five commitments go into every service agreement, with your checklist and your flat rate.

See the Costa Standard

  • Communication. One point of contact who knows your building. Replies average 8 minutes during office hours.
  • Accountability. Anything missed gets fixed, at no charge.
  • Dependability. The same trained crew every visit, with trained backups for sick days.
  • Quality. A room-by-room checklist completed every visit.
  • Accommodating. Cleaning before you open, after you close or on weekends.
